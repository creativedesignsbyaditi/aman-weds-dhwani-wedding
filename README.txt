Bride custom wedding site

1. Put your chosen MP3 in this folder and name it music.mp3.
2. Upload the entire folder contents to your GitHub Pages repository.
3. index.html is the entry point.
4. Envelope click starts music and opens the invitation.
5. Event videos autoplay when their section is visible.
6. TAP FOR WARDROBE opens the matching second video as a popup.
7. The arrow at top-right closes the popup.
